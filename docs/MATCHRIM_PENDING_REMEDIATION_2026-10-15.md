## Final Implementation Delta

- Food menus use two actual overlapping crops, plus a bounded central crop when
  width/height >=1.3. The first two side crops cut a three-panel board's middle
  text; the extra view preserves it. This is an aspect heuristic, NOT a learned
  column detector. Maximum3 concurrent model requests,30 returned rows per crop,
 90s total deadline; no full-scene preflight, paid retry or model fallback.
- Crop boxes are mapped back to original coordinates. Only literal agreement
  plus strong spatial overlap merges duplicate rows. Overlapping crop estimates
  are never summed into a fabricated global count. Coverage remains unverified.
- Conflicting dish names attached to the same literal row and strongly
  overlapping source boxes become one literal unresolved row, without an
  attributed pairing. Distinct positions/columns remain separate.
- Technical scan failures no longer inflate the wine-unrecognized total. The
  scene shows a separate error notice and keeps per-region manual retry.
- Wine-menu extraction also rejects truncated/prose/multi-object responses and
  malformed/oversized row arrays with typed nonretryable502 instead of repairing
  JSON or silently returning empty success. Invalid input is422. Unknown model
  counts remain null, not invented zero. Existing taste formulas are unchanged.
- Food suggestions remain style hypotheses, not restaurant stock, an identified
  bottle, personal food preference, dietary safety or a numerical affinity.
- Original fiche lookup now works even without an earlier local canonical ID;
  photo-inferred name/producer/vintage cannot certify an original record.
